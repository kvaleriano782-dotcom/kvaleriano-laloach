        About Kiyushi.com

        Kiyushi.com is a welcoming digital space for nursing students to learn, organize their notes, and stay motivated throughout their nursing journey.

        Think of it as a quiet study desk: a place to gather your notes, practice with flashcards, and take a breath between chapters.

        Study content on this site is placeholder material for practice and organization. It is not medical advice. Always follow your course materials, instructors, and clinical guidelines.
